# Capstone: "Describe what you want to build"

Track: 2, Engagement & Staying in the Learning Journey (with parts of Tracks 1 and 3).

## Full answer

I want to build Tether, a learning app that turns time on the phone into learning that sticks. It is for students preparing for any exam across Africa, and for busy people who need to understand a concept fast. The first version teaches one English concord move for Nigeria's JAMB exam.

**The problem.** Most people study with the answer close by. They feel ready, then struggle alone in the exam hall, and nobody shows them that gap.

**My approach.** Tether shows two scores: with help and alone. Short game-like rounds, a friendly coach and rechecks on day 3, 7 and 14 help close the gap. Fixed rules mark every answer; online AI explains the reviewed concept in simpler words when a learner is still stuck. The core learning loop works offline on cheap phones.

**What excites me.** Young people already love their phones and anything gamified. I want to channel that energy into real learning. It is alarming how much many students don't know, and this can help.
