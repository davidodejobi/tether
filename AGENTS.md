# Tether

Build an installable, offline study app for JAMB English concord, starting with
Move 1: Strip the Disguise. Deliver the full learning loop within 24 hours.

- Before planning or changing behaviour, read `docs/spec.md`, the current build
  contract. `docs/requirements.txt` and `docs/decisions.md` preserve its background.
- Follow `docs/roadmap.md` in order; update its completion checks as work lands.
- Keep this repository self-contained. Bring in planning documents, tokens and
  assets only when needed, and keep their paths local.
- Keep changes focused on the working learner loop; verify affected behaviour.
- Human-reviewed rules generate and grade answers. AI never grades.
- Never add AI attribution anywhere.
